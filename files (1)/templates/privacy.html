Privacy Policy

This policy is a template. If you launch this app for real users, please update it according to your country's laws (e.g., Bangladesh data protection regulations).

Information We Collect

During registration, we collect your name, address, date of birth, phone number, email (optional), and password. You may also provide a profile picture and bio if you wish.

Why We Collect It

To create your account, assign you an ID Number, and enable messaging features with other users.

Who Can See It

Other users can only see your name, ID Number, profile picture, bio, and online status. Your address, phone number, email, and date of birth are not visible to other users. App administrators can view registration details for all users. Passwords are stored in hashed format and cannot be viewed even by admins.

Messages

Messages are stored on the server. Message contents are not displayed in the admin panel. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted.

Your Rights

You can modify your information from your profile page. If you wish to delete your account, please contact an administrator.

কে দেখতে পায়
